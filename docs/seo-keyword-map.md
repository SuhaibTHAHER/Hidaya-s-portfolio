## Cannibalization rules

- The home pages (/ and /ar/) own the brand intent and the 'designer in Nablus/Palestine' intent. No other page may use 'Interior Designer in Nablus' or 'مصممة داخلية في نابلس' as its title or H1. Service pages use service + city ('Residential interior design in Nablus' / 'تصميم داخلي للمنازل في نابلس'), and project pages use project + type.
- Each search intent has one URL per language. Synonyms (تصميم داخلي, ديكور داخلي, مصممة ديكور, مصمم داخلي, مهندسة ديكور) are handled inside the page that owns the intent. Never create a separate page per synonym or per gendered form.
- Service pages own the 'hire for this type of space' intent. Project pages own the specific room or space-type long-tail. A project page links up to its service page with descriptive anchor text, and a service page links down to every project it covers. Project titles must never be generic ('Residential Design', 'Commercial Interior').
- Bedroom projects are split by modifier. Dressing area / walk-in closet / غرفة ملابس goes to bedroom-design. Master suite / غرفة نوم ماستر goes to master-bedroom. Shared siblings / gaming goes to kids-bedroom. Study wall / boy's room goes to boys-bedroom. Teal room with piano goes to private-room. Re-title the generic 'bedroom-design' page to 'Bedroom with Dressing Area' (the slug can stay to avoid redirects). The owner confirms which project actually contains the closet.
- Kitchen intents: 'مطبخ مفتوح على الصالة / مطبخ أمريكي' goes to /ar/projects/kitchen-and-dining-nook. Local 'تصميم مطابخ نابلس' goes to the kitchen section of the residential service page. There is no kitchen service page.
- The commercial service page holds retail, hospitality, office and bank sub-intents as sections. Do not split out retail or restaurant pages until GSC shows real impressions for those queries and the portfolio has more than one project of that type.
- Each FAQ answer lives on one page only. The render and plan-vs-render explainer is only on /services/3d-visualization and its Arabic mirror. Process, choosing a designer, design vs decor, pricing method and supervision scope are only on /process and its Arabic mirror. Home may show a one-line teaser with a link, never the full duplicated answer. FAQPage schema goes only on the page that holds the answer.
- Every EN/AR pair uses a self-referencing canonical, reciprocal hreflang (en, ar) and x-default pointing to English. Arabic pages are never canonicalized to English. Arabic must be a natural, complete Arabic page, not a partial translation that leaves English blocks behind. The 404 catch-alls under /ar/ must return real 404s.
- If a CV PDF is published (cvUrl is currently empty), serve it with an X-Robots-Tag noindex header, or host it on Issuu instead. Otherwise a PDF titled 'Interior Designer – Nablus' would compete with the home page for the same intent.
- Off-site copies (Behance, Facebook, Instagram, Khamsat, Mostaql, Issuu, Archello) must use shorter, differently worded descriptions and link back to the matching page on the site. Do not paste the full case-study text, so the site stays the original and most complete version.
- Nablus is the only city in titles and H1s. Other cities or 'West Bank' appear only in one factual service-area sentence, and only if the owner confirms she really serves them.

## Pages deliberately NOT created (and why)

- Separate city or area landing pages (Ramallah, Jenin, Tulkarm, 'West Bank', 'Palestine-wide'). She is based in Nablus, so these would be doorway pages. 'interior design West Bank' returned homonyms (Westbank BC, South Africa) and political content.
- A page targeting 'interior visualization Palestine'. The Arabic and English SERPs were taken over by the 'Visualizing Palestine' nonprofit; use '3D interior visualization' + Nablus on the 3D page instead.
- A kitchen design service page. Only 1 kitchen project exists, it would be thin, and it would duplicate the residential page and the kitchen project page.
- A standalone bedroom design or kids' room service page. It would duplicate the residential page; the 5 bedroom projects already have their own case studies.
- Separate retail, restaurant/café, office or bank service pages. There are only 1 to 3 projects per type, and the intent overlaps the commercial page. Revisit only with more projects and GSC evidence.
- A separate /about page. Brand and local intent already belong to the home page, which contains the about section; a second page would split the name and 'designer in Nablus' signals.
- A 'female interior designer Nablus' landing page. The intent is the same as home; state the fact naturally in the about copy.
- 'Interior design company / شركة ديكور / مكتب تصميم داخلي' pages. She is a freelancer without a company or office. 'شركة ديكور نابلس' and 'ديكورات نابلس' also lean toward execution trades (gypsum, carpet, contractors). Directories are where those queries are won.
- 'مهندسة ديكور' title pages. She holds a Bachelor of Interior Design, not an engineering degree; use the term only as a synonym inside a truthful FAQ answer.
- A majlis or 'تصميم مجالس' page. The intent is Gulf, the cultural fit is poor, and there is no majlis project.
- Generic head-term guides ('تصميم غرف نوم', 'أفكار ديكور حديثة', 'أنماط التصميم الداخلي', 'common interior design styles', 'interior design portfolio examples'). Publishers, IKEA and website builders own them, the searchers are not clients, and they would be thin compared with those competitors.
- Style or tag archive pages (/projects/style/classical etc.). With 1–2 projects per style they would be thin, duplicate listings.
- A pricing page with figures, unless the owner supplies real rates. Never borrow Saudi or Egyptian per-metre prices.
- 'Palestinian identity in contemporary interiors' article (both languages). An-Najah papers and Coohom posts rank for it, but nothing in the portfolio shows Palestinian heritage elements. Create it only if the owner has real work or expertise on the topic.
- Supervision, execution or turnkey service pages, or before/after galleries. Nothing verified says she executes projects, and all 14 projects are renders. Create these only if the owner confirms, with real built work.
- Exterior or landscape design pages. These are not verified services.
- Arabic-slug duplicates of existing pages (e.g. /ar/تصميم-داخلي-نابلس alongside /ar/). They would be exact duplicates and add encoding risk.

- 'تصميم داخلي' is the formal term used by universities and directories: shobiddak's category 'محلات وشركات تصميم داخلي', program names 'التصميم الداخلي والديكور' (paluniv, PTUK) and An-Najah's 'قسم التصميم الداخلي والديكور'. In standard mode its SERPs are dominated by academic and education pages. Use it in titles, together with a commercial word (خدمات, مصممة, نابلس).
- 'ديكور' is the everyday word clients use. 'مصممة ديكور' appears in Nablus job ads ('مطلوب مصممة ديكور في نابلس' on dooz.ps) and in Instagram titles ('مصممة ديكور داخلي'). Used alone, though ('ديكورات نابلس', 'شركة ديكور نابلس'), it brings up execution trades: gypsum, wood, stone, carpet, contractors. Best phrasing: 'تصميم ديكور داخلي' / 'مصممة ديكور داخلي'. 'ديكور داخلي نابلس' had zero Nablus providers in the results.
- 'مصممة داخلية' (feminine) is the exact wording of the CV that ranked first for 'مصممة داخلية في نابلس'. Use the feminine forms consistently because she is a woman. Also use the generic masculine 'مصمم داخلي' once, naturally (e.g. 'إذا كنت تبحث عن مصمم داخلي في نابلس'), because clients often search with the masculine form.
- 'مهندسة ديكور' / 'مهندس ديكور' are common colloquial terms, but they also bring up job boards (tanqeeb), courses ('دورة هندسة ديكور') and news. She holds a Bachelor of Interior Design, not an engineering degree, so never use them as a title. Mention them once in an FAQ explaining that people often say مهندسة ديكور.
- For living and reception rooms, use the Levantine 'صالون' / 'غرفة جلوس' / 'صالة جلوس'. 'مجالس' brings up Gulf majlis content, and 'تصميم صالات' is ambiguous (event halls, dining halls, beauty salons).
- Kitchen: use both 'مطبخ مفتوح على الصالة' and 'مطبخ أمريكي' (a frequent synonym in titles). Bedroom: 'غرفة نوم ماستر' and 'غرفة نوم رئيسية'; 'غرفة ملابس' and 'دريسنج'. Kids: 'غرفة أطفال مشتركة', 'غرفة نوم أولاد' and 'غرف نوم الشباب'. The bare 'غرف نوم اطفال' / 'غرفة نوم اولاد' means buying furniture.
- 3D vocabulary: 'رندر' (also spelled 'ريندر'), 'إظهار' (the academic/professional term, with hamza), 'تصميم ثلاثي الأبعاد' and Latin '3D' are all in use, often mixed in one title ('تصميم ديكور 3D'). Use several of them naturally on the visualization page.
- Fix ambiguous phrasings: 'محل كاميرات تصوير' or 'معرض كاميرات' instead of 'محل كاميرات' (read as CCTV); 'ريسبشن' or 'كاونتر استقبال' instead of 'تصميم استقبال' (read as radio receivers or a home reception room). - Local apartment vocabulary from Nablus listings: 'شقة عظم' (shell), 'مشطبة' (finished), 'تشطيب', 'ديكور جبصين'. These are useful for the residential section on design before finishing.
- Avoid 'الضفة الغربية' as a target (it is unnatural for clients and brought up political and 'western-style' content). Country-level 'فلسطين' queries mix in study intent (دبلوم, تخصص); always pair فلسطين with نابلس and a service word.
- Name: the site uses 'هداية ظاهر' (alternate 'هدايه ظاهر'), but no external source publishes her Arabic spelling. The bare name collides with religious vocabulary (هداية = guidance, ظاهر = apparent), so always pair it with 'مصممة داخلية'. The owner must confirm ظاهر vs طاهر.
- All of these observations come from a US-localized, non-Google tool. How often Palestinians actually use each term is not verified; compare the terms in GSC (Palestine filter) after launch and adjust titles.
